01

Introduction

PhotoPro AI is an Android photo editing application developed by NivAk Technologies.

The application may provide traditional photo editing tools, creative tools and AI-powered image features.

You do not currently need to create a PhotoPro AI account to use the application.

02

Photos and Images

PhotoPro AI may allow you to select photos and images from your device or capture new images using your device camera.

Images may be used for functions such as:

  • Photo editing
  • Crop, rotate and resize
  • Filters and adjustments
  • Collages and creative tools
  • Background editing
  • Image enhancement
  • AI-powered image processing
  • Saving and exporting edited images

You retain ownership of photos and images that you provide to PhotoPro AI.

03

AI-Powered Image Processing

Some PhotoPro AI features may use artificial intelligence or remote image-processing services.

When an AI or online feature requires remote processing, the selected image and information required to perform the requested operation may be securely transmitted to a remote processing service.

AI-powered features may include functions such as:

  • AI image enhancement
  • Background removal or replacement
  • Object removal
  • Upscaling
  • Denoising and deblurring
  • Photo restoration
  • Portrait enhancement and retouching
  • Image generation
  • Style transformation
  • Relighting and other AI editing functions

Features that do not require remote processing may be performed locally on the device.

The exact processing provider may change as PhotoPro AI is developed. This Privacy Policy will be updated when production AI providers are finalized.

04

Information Stored Locally

PhotoPro AI may store information locally on your device to provide app functionality.

This may include:

  • Your chosen display name
  • App preferences
  • Points or reward balances
  • Reward claim history
  • Feature preferences
  • Temporary editing information

Local information may be removed when app storage is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

05

Device Permissions

PhotoPro AI may request Android permissions or use Android system features needed for photo editing.

Permission or feature Purpose
Photos and media Select images for editing.
Camera Capture a new photo for editing.
Files or storage Open images and save or export edited images where Android requires access.
Internet Access AI services, Firebase, advertising, support pages and other online functionality.

Permission names and behavior may vary depending on your Android version and device manufacturer.

06

Firebase Services

PhotoPro AI uses Google Firebase services.

Firebase Remote Config may be used to control feature availability, configuration values, rewards and other application behavior without requiring a new app release.

Additional Firebase services may be introduced before or after release. This Privacy Policy will be updated when necessary.

07

Advertising and Consent

PhotoPro AI may display advertisements using Google AdMob.

Advertising services may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • Approximate technical information
  • Ad interactions
  • Diagnostics and fraud-prevention information

Where required by law, PhotoPro AI will request consent before using personal data for personalized advertising.

Privacy choices may be managed through the consent or privacy options made available in the application.

08

Third-Party Services

PhotoPro AI may interact with third-party services, including:

  • Google Play
  • Google Firebase
  • Google AdMob
  • Android photo and media services
  • Android camera services
  • Android sharing and file services
  • AI and image-processing providers

Third-party services operate according to their own privacy policies, terms and security practices.

09

Data Security

NivAk Technologies uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information processed through PhotoPro AI.

When remote processing is required, information is intended to be transmitted using secure network connections.

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10

Data Retention

Information stored locally remains on your device until you remove it, clear app storage, or uninstall PhotoPro AI.

Edited images saved or exported to your device remain there until you delete them.

Images sent to remote AI or image-processing services may be retained only according to the processing requirements and policies of the applicable service provider.

Specific provider retention information will be added when the production AI processing provider is finalized.

11

Delete Your Data

You can remove locally stored PhotoPro AI information by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the application.

Photos and edited images exported outside the application must be deleted separately from the location where they were saved.

View Data Deletion Guide Contact Support
12

Children's Privacy

PhotoPro AI is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

NivAk Technologies does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information through a support or online service, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

13

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PhotoPro AI features, permissions, AI providers, Firebase services, advertising systems or legal requirements change.

The Last updated date on this page will be revised when material changes are made.
