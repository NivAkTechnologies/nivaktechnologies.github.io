Delete Your Data

This guide explains how to delete saved resumes, profile information, exported PDF documents and eligible support information related to Resume Builder.

01

Overview

Resume Builder does not currently require users to create an online account.

Most profile and resume information is stored locally on the Android device.

Resume Builder data may include:

  • Profile information
  • Saved resume drafts
  • Completed resumes
  • Template selections
  • Profile photo references
  • App settings and preferences
  • Exported PDF documents
  • Support emails or contact information
02

Delete an Individual Saved Resume

You can remove a saved resume directly from the My Resumes screen.

  1. Open Resume Builder.
  2. Open My Resumes.
  3. Find the resume you want to remove.
  4. Open the resume menu.
  5. Select Delete.
  6. Confirm the deletion.

Deleting a saved resume is intended to remove that resume from local app storage. This action may not be reversible.

03

Remove or Update Profile Information

Resume Builder may use saved profile information to prefill new resumes.

Where available, open the Profile section and remove, replace or update the saved information.

This may include:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Date of birth
  • Address
  • Nationality
  • Profile photograph

If the current version does not provide a complete profile-reset option, clearing the app's Android storage removes locally stored profile information.

04

Delete Exported PDF Documents

Exported PDF files are stored in the location selected by the user or chosen by Android.

To remove an exported resume:

  1. Open Android Files or another file manager.
  2. Open Downloads, Documents or the selected folder.
  3. Find the exported resume PDF.
  4. Select Delete.
  5. Empty the device recycle bin if applicable.

Deleting a resume inside the app does not necessarily delete PDF copies previously exported to device storage.

PDF copies shared to cloud storage, email or other apps must be deleted separately from those services.

05

Clear Resume Builder Storage in Android

You can remove all locally stored Resume Builder app data through Android settings.

  1. Open your device Settings.
  2. Open Apps or Applications.
  3. Select Resume Builder.
  4. Open Storage or Storage & cache.
  5. Select Clear storage, Clear data or the equivalent option.
  6. Confirm the action.

Clearing app storage may permanently remove all saved profiles, resume drafts, completed resumes, template selections and app preferences stored locally.

Menu names may differ depending on Android version and device manufacturer.

06

Uninstall Resume Builder

Uninstalling Resume Builder generally removes its locally stored application data from the device.

Uninstalling does not automatically delete:

  • PDF files exported outside the app
  • Files saved in cloud storage
  • Resume files shared to another application
  • Support emails previously sent by the user
  • Eligible support records stored by future online support services
07

Support and Contact Information

If you contact NivAk Technologies by email or through a future in-app Contact Us feature, the support record may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message content
  • Submission date and time
  • App identification
  • Device details voluntarily provided
  • Screenshots voluntarily attached

You may request deletion of eligible support information associated with your email address.

08

How to Submit a Data Deletion Request

Send an email to:

Email nivaktechnologies@outlook.com
Subject Resume Builder Data Deletion Request

Include:

  • The email address used when contacting support
  • The approximate date of the support enquiry
  • A brief description that helps locate the record
  • A clear statement requesting deletion

Do not send passwords, bank details, identity documents or complete resume files unless specifically requested and appropriate.

Email Deletion Request Contact Page
09

Verification and Retention

NivAk Technologies may request reasonable information to verify that the requester is associated with the support record.

Eligible information will be reviewed and deleted where required and reasonably possible.

Some information may need to be retained temporarily where necessary for:

  • Legal obligations
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Resolving an active dispute
  • Protecting legal rights
  • Maintaining essential support records

Locally stored resume information cannot be remotely deleted by NivAk Technologies because it remains on the user's device.
