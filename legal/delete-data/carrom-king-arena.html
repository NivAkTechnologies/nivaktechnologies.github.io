01

Overview

Carrom King Arena stores some information locally on your Android device and may also use Firebase services to support player identity, progression, rankings, lives, wallet features, matches and other online-supported functionality.

Because some information may be stored through Firebase, uninstalling the game or clearing local app data may not remove all information associated with supported online services.

02

Delete Local Game Data

You can remove locally stored Carrom King Arena data through Android settings.

Android menu names may vary depending on your device manufacturer and Android version.

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Open Apps or Applications.
  3. Select Carrom King Arena.
  4. Open Storage or Storage & cache.
  5. Select Clear storage or Clear data.

You may also uninstall Carrom King Arena to remove local data stored by the application.

Local data may include:

  • Cached settings
  • Player preferences
  • Game configuration
  • Local progression information
  • Other locally stored gameplay information

Clearing app storage or uninstalling the game does not necessarily remove Firebase Authentication, Firestore or other server-side information.

03

Firebase and Server-Side Data

Carrom King Arena may use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and related Firebase services to operate supported game features.

Server-side information may include:

  • Firebase-generated player identifier
  • Player profile information
  • Game progress
  • Match records and results
  • Scores and ranking information
  • Lives and restoration information
  • Virtual wallet and reward balances
  • Challenge and progression information
  • Game configuration and feature state
  • Technical timestamps and service information
04

Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of eligible Carrom King Arena player or backend data, contact NivAk Technologies using the details below.

Email support@nivak-technologies.com
Subject Carrom King Arena Data Deletion Request

We may request information reasonably necessary to identify the relevant player record and process the request.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive information with your deletion request.

05

Data We Can Delete

Where applicable and where the relevant player record can be reasonably identified, NivAk Technologies may delete eligible information such as:

  • Player profile information
  • Game progression data
  • Match and ranking information
  • Lives and reward information
  • Virtual wallet balances
  • Challenge and progression records
  • Associated Firebase player records

Local application data can be removed directly from the device using Android settings.

06

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, fraud prevention, dispute handling, legal compliance or the establishment and defence of legal claims.

Where retention is required, access will be limited as appropriate and information will not be kept longer than reasonably necessary for that purpose.

07

Google and Advertising Data

Carrom King Arena may use Google Firebase, Google Play and advertising services.

These third-party services may process device identifiers, IP address, application information, advertising identifiers and information about advertisement interactions.

NivAk Technologies does not directly control all information processed independently by Google.

Information held directly by Google may need to be managed through your Google account, Android privacy settings, advertising settings or Google's own privacy tools.
