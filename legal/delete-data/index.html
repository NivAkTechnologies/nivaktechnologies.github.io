Delete Your Data

This page explains how to remove data stored locally by QuickCalc Daily and how to request deletion of eligible support messages submitted through the Contact Us feature.

01

Overview

QuickCalc Daily does not require users to create an account. Most app data is stored locally on the Android device.

Data associated with QuickCalc Daily may include:

  • Calculator history
  • Favourite calculators
  • Theme and app preferences
  • Other locally stored settings
  • Support messages submitted through Contact Us
02

Delete Local App Data

Calculator history, favourites and app settings are stored locally on your device.

Where available, you may remove individual items or clear supported data from within the application.

The exact controls available inside the app may change as QuickCalc Daily continues through testing and development.

03

Clear QuickCalc Daily Storage in Android

You can remove locally stored app data through Android settings.

  1. Open your device Settings.
  2. Open Apps or Applications.
  3. Select QuickCalc Daily.
  4. Open Storage or Storage & cache.
  5. Choose Clear storage, Clear data or the equivalent option.
  6. Confirm the action.

Clearing app storage permanently removes locally stored QuickCalc Daily history, favourites and settings from that device.

Menu names may vary depending on the Android version and device manufacturer.

04

Uninstall QuickCalc Daily

Uninstalling QuickCalc Daily generally removes its locally stored application data from the device.

Removing the app does not automatically delete support messages that were previously submitted to Firebase Firestore.

05

Delete Contact or Support Messages

Messages submitted through the Contact Us feature may be stored in Google Firebase Firestore.

A stored support record may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message content
  • Submission date and time
  • App identification
  • Internal support status

You may request deletion of an eligible support record by contacting NivAk Technologies.

06

How to Submit a Deletion Request

Send an email to:

Email nivaktechnologies@outlook.com
Subject QuickCalc Daily Data Deletion Request

Include:

  • The email address used in the Contact Us form
  • The approximate date the message was submitted
  • A brief description that helps locate the message
  • A clear statement that you want the eligible support record deleted

Do not send passwords, bank details, identity documents or unnecessary sensitive information.

Email Deletion Request Contact Page
07

Verification and Retention

NivAk Technologies may request reasonable information to verify that the person making the request is associated with the support message.

Eligible data will be reviewed and deleted where required and reasonably possible.

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where necessary for:

  • Legal obligations
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Resolving an active dispute
  • Protecting legal rights

Data that cannot reasonably be linked to the information provided in the request may not be identifiable for deletion.

08

Contact NivAk Technologies

Contact us if you need help understanding this guide or submitting a deletion request.
