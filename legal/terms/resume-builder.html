About Resume Builder

Resume Builder is an Android productivity application developed by NivAk Technologies.

The application may allow users to:

  • Create and edit professional resumes
  • Enter profile and career information
  • Add education and employment history
  • Add skills, projects and certificates
  • Select resume templates
  • Save and manage multiple resumes
  • Generate PDF documents
  • Share or print exported resumes

Features may change as the application is developed, tested and updated.
