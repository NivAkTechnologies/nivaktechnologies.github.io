Overview

Everyday calculations without unnecessary complexity.

QuickCalc Daily brings useful everyday calculators into one organized application. It is designed for speed, clarity and easy access, whether users are working with money, measurements, percentages or daily planning.

Calculation history, favourites, PDF export, sharing, light and dark themes and a modular calculator structure make the app practical today and ready for future expansion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about QuickCalc Daily.

Is QuickCalc Daily publicly available?

QuickCalc Daily is currently in Google Play closed testing and is not yet publicly released.

Does the app require an internet connection?

Core calculator functions are designed to work offline. Some support or online services may require an internet connection.

Can calculation results be exported?

Supported calculations can be exported or shared as PDF documents through compatible apps on the device.

Can users delete their locally stored data?

Yes. Users can clear app data through the app where available or through Android application settings.

How can users contact support?

Users can access the NivAk Technologies Support and Contact pages using the links on this website.
