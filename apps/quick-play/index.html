Overview

Play fast mini-games built around reaction speed, memory, colour and number challenges.

Quick Play brings several short mini-games together in one colourful app designed for quick sessions and repeat play.

Players can jump between different game modes, improve their scores and challenge themselves with simple rules and fast rounds.

The first release focuses on Memory Match, Colour Match, Speed Tap and Number Match, with room for more mini-games in future updates.

Privacy & Control

Game data and purchases are handled transparently.

Quick Play stores core game progress and settings locally. Online services are used only where needed for supported features described in the Privacy Policy.

Read the Quick Play Privacy Policy →
  • Quick Play does not require a traditional user account for normal gameplay.
  • Core game progress, settings and gameplay data can be stored locally on the device.
  • Players can access Quick Play data-deletion information through the website.
  • Supported Google Play and online services are handled only when required by enabled game features.
  • Future mini-games can be added through the app's modular game structure.
Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Quick Play.

Is Quick Play publicly available?

Quick Play is currently nearly complete and being prepared for release.

Does Quick Play require an account?

No. Quick Play is designed for direct gameplay without a traditional sign-up flow.

Where is game progress stored?

Core game progress and settings can be stored locally on the Android device, while selected protected data such as verified purchase information may be handled through Firebase backend services.

Which games are currently included?

The current game collection includes Memory Match, Colour Match, Speed Tap and Number Match, with additional mini-games planned for future updates.

Will more mini-games be added?

Yes. Quick Play uses a modular structure designed to support additional mini-games in future versions.
