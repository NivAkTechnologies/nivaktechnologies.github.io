Overview

Solve colourful water sort puzzles across multiple difficulty levels.

ColourFlow Master combines relaxing water sort gameplay with progressive puzzle difficulty, rewards and long-term progression.

Players can jump between different game modes, improve their scores and challenge themselves with simple rules and fast rounds.

The first release includes Easy, Medium and Hard puzzle levels, with additional game modes planned for future updates.

Privacy & Control

Game data and purchases are handled transparently.

ColourFlow Master stores core game progress and settings locally. Online services are used only where needed for supported features described in the Privacy Policy.

Read the ColourFlow Master Privacy Policy →
  • ColourFlow Master does not require a traditional user account for normal gameplay.
  • Core game progress, settings and gameplay data can be stored locally on the device.
  • Players can access ColourFlow Master data-deletion information through the website.
  • Supported Google Play and online services are handled only when required by enabled game features.
  • Future game modes and puzzle content can be added through the app's modular structure.
Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about ColourFlow Master.

Is ColourFlow Master publicly available?

ColourFlow Master is currently nearly complete and being prepared for release.

Does ColourFlow Master require an account?

No. ColourFlow Master is designed for direct gameplay without a traditional sign-up flow.

Where is game progress stored?

Core game progress and settings can be stored locally on the Android device, while selected protected data such as verified purchase information may be handled through Firebase backend services.

Which games are currently included?

The current game includes Easy, Medium and Hard water sort puzzle levels, with additional modes planned for future updates.

Will more game modes be added?

Yes. ColourFlow Master uses a modular structure designed to support additional game modes and puzzle content in future versions.
