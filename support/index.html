QuickCalc Daily FAQ

Common app questions.

Is QuickCalc Daily publicly available?

QuickCalc Daily is currently in Google Play closed testing. It is not yet publicly released.

Do I need an account?

No. QuickCalc Daily does not currently require users to create an account or sign in.

Where are history and favourites stored?

Calculation history, favourites and app settings are stored locally on the Android device.

Why is a calculator result different from another app?

Differences may come from user input, rounding, decimal precision, formulas or assumptions. Review the entered values and verify important results before relying on them.

How do I report a problem?

Email support@nivak-technologies.com with the subject “QuickCalc Daily Support”. Include the calculator name, steps that caused the problem and screenshots where useful.

PDF Help

Exporting and sharing documents.

How do I export a PDF?

Open a supported calculator, complete the required values and use the available PDF export option. The exact button location may depend on the calculator and app version.

Why does the PDF not open?

Confirm that a PDF viewer is installed. Try opening the file from Android Files or another compatible PDF application.

Why does sharing fail?

Confirm the selected sharing app supports PDF files, has the required connection and is working normally. Try saving the PDF first and sharing it from the Android Files app.

Where is the exported file saved?

The location depends on Android, the selected export method and the file-handling app used by the device. Check Downloads, Documents or the location selected during export.

Can I print an exported PDF?

Yes, when a compatible PDF viewer and Android print service are available. Open the PDF and choose Print from the viewer menu.
