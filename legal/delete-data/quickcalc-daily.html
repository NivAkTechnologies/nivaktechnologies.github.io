01

Overview

Calculator – Simple & Smart does not require you to create an account or sign in.

The app stores some information locally on your Android device and may store limited support information in Firebase Firestore when you voluntarily use the Contact Us feature.

Advertising is provided through Google AdMob. Advertising information handled by Google is subject to Google's own systems and policies.

02

Delete Local App Data

You can remove locally stored Calculator – Simple & Smart data directly through Android.

Android menu names may differ slightly depending on your device manufacturer and Android version.

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Open Apps or Applications.
  3. Select Calculator – Simple & Smart.
  4. Open Storage or Storage & cache.
  5. Select Clear storage or Clear data.

You may also uninstall the app to remove local data stored by the application.

Local data may include items such as:

  • Calculation history
  • Saved favourites
  • App settings
  • Theme preferences
  • Other locally stored calculator information

Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes locally stored application data, but it does not automatically delete support messages already submitted to NivAk Technologies.

03

Support and Contact Data

If you use the Contact Us feature, information you voluntarily submit may be stored in Firebase Firestore so NivAk Technologies can receive and respond to your enquiry.

That information may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your support or contact message
  • Submission date and time
  • App identification
  • Internal support status
04

Request Deletion

To request deletion of eligible support information held by NivAk Technologies, email:

Email support@nivak-technologies.com
Subject QuickCalc Data Deletion Request

Please provide enough information for us to identify the support record you want deleted, such as the email address used when contacting us and an approximate date of the message.

Do not send passwords, payment card details, identity documents or other sensitive information with your deletion request.

05

Data We Can Delete

Where applicable and where we can reasonably identify the relevant record, NivAk Technologies may delete support information such as:

  • Name submitted in a support request
  • Email address submitted in a support request
  • Support or contact message content
  • Associated support metadata

Local application data can be removed directly by you from the Android device as described above.

06

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may be retained where reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, maintain security or establish and defend legal claims.

Where retention is required, access will be limited as appropriate and the information will not be kept longer than reasonably necessary for that purpose.

07

Google and AdMob Data

Calculator – Simple & Smart uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google may process device information, advertising-related identifiers, IP address, general location information and information about ad interactions.

NivAk Technologies does not directly control or maintain all advertising information processed by Google.

Requests concerning information held directly by Google may need to be managed through your Google account, device privacy settings, advertising settings or Google's own privacy tools.
