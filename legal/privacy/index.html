01

Introduction

NivAk Technologies respects the privacy of visitors to this website. This policy applies to the NivAk Technologies website and does not replace the separate privacy policies provided for individual NivAk Technologies apps and games.

02

Information We Receive

You can browse the website without creating an account. We may receive personal information only when you choose to provide it, for example when contacting NivAk Technologies.

Information you provide may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The content of your message
  • Any other information you voluntarily include
03

How Information Is Used

Information you provide may be used to respond to enquiries, provide support, communicate about NivAk Technologies products, investigate reported website problems and maintain website security.

NivAk Technologies does not sell personal information to advertisers.

04

Technical Information

Like most websites, hosting and infrastructure providers may process standard technical information required to deliver and protect the website. This can include information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request time and requested page.

This information may be used for website delivery, reliability, security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting.

05

Third-Party Links

The website may contain links to third-party services such as Google Play or other external websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by their respective operators and are not covered by this Website Privacy Policy.

06

Data Retention

Information voluntarily submitted to NivAk Technologies is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received, including support, communication, security and applicable record-keeping requirements.

07

Your Choices

You may contact NivAk Technologies to ask questions about personal information you have provided through the website or to request appropriate access, correction or deletion.

App-specific data requests should use the privacy or data-deletion information provided for the relevant app or game.

08

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Website Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, services or applicable requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date.
