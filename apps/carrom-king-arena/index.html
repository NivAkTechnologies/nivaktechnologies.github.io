Overview

Classic carrom gameplay with multiple ways to play, compete and progress.

Carrom King Arena brings the familiar carrom-board experience to Android with polished controls and a premium game presentation.

Play against the computer, challenge another player locally, enter multiplayer matches or take on dedicated challenges.

Rankings, rewards and the in-game shop add progression beyond individual matches.
