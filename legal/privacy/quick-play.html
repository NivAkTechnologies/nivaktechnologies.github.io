01

Introduction

NivAk Technologies respects your privacy and aims to explain clearly how Quick Play handles data.

Quick Play is an Android casual game that may include multiple mini-games, player progress, virtual coins, gems, experience points, achievements, daily rewards and advertisements.

Quick Play does not require you to create an online account using your email address, phone number or password.

02

Game and Player Data

Quick Play stores information needed to provide gameplay, player progression, rewards and profile features.

This may include:

  • Player display name
  • Selected avatar
  • Game progress and scores
  • Virtual coin and gem balances
  • Experience points and player progression
  • Achievements and reward status
  • Daily reward information
  • Reward and wallet history
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Information used to prevent duplicate rewards

This player and gameplay information is currently stored locally on your Android device using app storage.

Quick Play does not currently upload your player name, avatar or locally stored game progress to a Quick Play user account or Firebase database.

03

Advertising and Rewarded Ads

Quick Play uses Google Mobile Ads / Google AdMob to provide advertising, including rewarded ads.

Rewarded ads are optional game interactions where available. A player may choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game reward such as coins or lives.

Google and its advertising partners may process information required to deliver, measure, secure and, where permitted, personalize advertisements.

Depending on your device, region and consent choices, this may include information such as:

  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from technical signals
  • Ad impressions and interactions
  • Device and app information
  • Fraud-prevention and diagnostic information

Where required, consent or privacy choices may be presented before advertising data is used for certain purposes.

04

Firebase Services

Quick Play uses Google Firebase Core and Firebase Remote Config to support application configuration and feature management.

Firebase Remote Config may be used to control features such as:

  • Game availability
  • Reward availability and reward amounts
  • Daily reward settings
  • Advertisement availability
  • Banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisement settings
  • Game status and coming-soon features

Firebase Remote Config may process technical app and device information required to retrieve configuration values and operate the service.

Quick Play does not currently use Firebase Authentication, Firebase Firestore or Firebase Cloud Functions for player accounts or game-progress storage.

05

Local Device Storage

Quick Play stores player and gameplay information locally on your Android device so that progress and preferences can be preserved between game sessions.

Local information may include:

  • Player display name and avatar
  • Game progress and scores
  • Coins, gems and experience points
  • Achievements and rewards
  • Daily reward status
  • Wallet and reward history
  • Game settings and preferences

Clearing Quick Play's app storage or uninstalling the game may permanently remove this locally stored information.

08

Support and Contact Enquiries

If you contact NivAk Technologies by email or through the website, information you voluntarily provide may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your support message
  • Device or Android version information
  • Screenshots or other files you choose to provide

This information is used to respond to your enquiry, investigate problems and provide support.

Do not send passwords, complete payment card details or other unnecessary sensitive information when contacting support.

09

Third-Party Services

Quick Play relies on third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

These may include:

  • Google Play
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads

NivAk Technologies does not control the independent privacy practices of these third-party services.

10

Data Security

NivAk Technologies uses reasonable technical measures intended to protect Quick Play and its supporting services.

Measures used by the app may include secure communication with Firebase services and privacy controls provided for advertising where required.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11

Data Retention

Local game data generally remains on the device until it is deleted by the user, cleared by Android or removed when the app is uninstalled.

Locally stored player and gameplay information generally remains on the device until it is cleared by the user, removed by Android or deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Some records may need to be retained after a deletion request when retention is required for security, fraud prevention, transaction records or applicable law.

12

Delete Your Data

You may remove local Quick Play information by clearing the app's storage in Android or uninstalling the app.

Quick Play currently stores player profile and gameplay information locally on your Android device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app may remove this locally stored information.

You may also contact NivAk Technologies if you have questions about data deletion, advertising privacy or information handled through support enquiries.

We may request information reasonably necessary to understand and respond to your deletion or privacy request.

13

Children's Privacy

Quick Play is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect a child's name, email address or other directly entered personal profile information.

Advertising availability and privacy treatment may vary depending on age, region, Google Play settings and applicable legal requirements.

Parents or guardians who believe information relating to a child has been processed inappropriately may contact NivAk Technologies for review.

Updated information will be published on this page and the Last updated date will be revised.
