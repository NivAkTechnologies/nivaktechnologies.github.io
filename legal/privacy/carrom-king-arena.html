01

Introduction

NivAk Technologies respects your privacy and aims to explain clearly how Carrom King Arena handles information.

Carrom King Arena is an Android carrom game with game modes, player progression, lives, rewards, wallet features, rankings and online-supported functionality.

The game may use Firebase services and local device storage to provide gameplay, maintain player progress and operate supported online features.

02

Game and Player Data

Carrom King Arena may process information required to provide player accounts, gameplay, progression and game services.

This may include:

  • Anonymous or Firebase-generated user identifiers
  • Player profile information
  • Game progress and match information
  • Scores, results and ranking-related information
  • Lives and life-restoration information
  • Virtual wallet and reward balances
  • Challenge and progression information
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Feature availability and configuration information
  • Technical information needed to operate online services

The exact information processed depends on the features used and the current version of the game.

03

Firebase Services

Carrom King Arena uses Google Firebase services to support selected game features.

These services may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Cloud Functions
  • Firebase Remote Config
  • Firebase Core services

Firebase Authentication may be used to create and maintain a player identity required for protected online features.

Cloud Firestore may be used to store or retrieve supported player and game information.

Firebase Cloud Functions may be used to process protected game operations such as player, wallet, lives or match-related requests.

Firebase Remote Config is used to manage configuration such as feature availability, maintenance states, game settings and other remotely controlled values.

Google may process technical information necessary to provide these Firebase services in accordance with Google's own privacy terms.

04

Local Device Storage

Carrom King Arena uses local device storage for selected application information and preferences.

This may include:

  • Startup and application configuration
  • Cached settings
  • Player preferences
  • Game configuration information
  • Other data required to preserve supported app behaviour

Some local information may be stored using Android application storage and SharedPreferences.

Clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the game may remove locally stored information.

05

Advertising

Carrom King Arena contains advertising-related components designed to support advertisements in parts of the game.

Advertising availability may vary by application version, feature configuration, region and release status.

When advertising services are enabled, the advertising provider may process technical information required to deliver, secure and measure advertisements.

This may include information such as:

  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Device and application information
  • Advertisement impressions or interactions
  • Fraud-prevention and diagnostic information

Where required by law, appropriate consent or privacy choices may be presented before advertising data is used for certain purposes.

06

Support and Contact Enquiries

If you contact NivAk Technologies through email or the website, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide.

This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your support message
  • Device or Android version information
  • Screenshots or files you choose to provide

This information is used to respond to your enquiry, investigate problems and provide support.

Do not send passwords, full payment-card information or other unnecessary sensitive information when contacting support.

07

Third-Party Services

Carrom King Arena relies on third-party platforms and services that may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

These may include:

  • Google Play
  • Google Firebase
  • Google advertising services when advertising is enabled

NivAk Technologies does not control the independent privacy practices of these third-party services.

Google Privacy Policy
08

Data Security

NivAk Technologies uses reasonable technical measures intended to protect information handled through Carrom King Arena and its supporting services.

Protected online game operations may be handled through Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

09

Data Retention

Locally stored information generally remains on your device until it is cleared, removed by Android or deleted when the application is uninstalled.

Information stored through Firebase services may remain for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the relevant game feature, maintain game integrity, provide support or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Some records may need to be retained where required for security, abuse prevention, dispute handling or applicable law.

10

Delete Your Data

You may remove locally stored Carrom King Arena information by clearing the application's storage through Android settings or uninstalling the game.

Because Carrom King Arena may use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and other backend services, uninstalling the application may not by itself remove information stored through supported online services.

If you would like to request deletion of information associated with Carrom King Arena's online services, contact NivAk Technologies through the website or email address below.

We may request information reasonably necessary to identify the relevant game record and process your privacy request.

Request Data Deletion Email Support
11

Children's Privacy

Carrom King Arena is intended for a general audience and is not designed to require children to provide a name, email address, phone number or other directly entered contact information for ordinary gameplay.

Privacy treatment and advertising availability may vary according to age, region, Google Play settings and applicable legal requirements.

Parents or guardians who believe information relating to a child has been processed inappropriately may contact NivAk Technologies for review.

12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Carrom King Arena's features, Firebase services, advertising, data practices or legal requirements change.

Updated information will be published on this page and the Last updated date will be revised.
